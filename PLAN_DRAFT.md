## Abs — daily block

Rules:
- 3 exercises per day, floor only (no equipment), done **last** in the session.
- Each day = 1 upper abs + 1 lower abs + 1 obliques/stability.
- Push days (1, 3, 5): lying-down moves only — shoulders/triceps are fatigued from pressing, so no planks.
- Pull + Legs days (2, 4, 6): planks allowed (pressing muscles fresh); no mountain climbers after leg work.
- No repeats across the week — 18 different ab moves.
